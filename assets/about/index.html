ABOUT ECHOES

A WORLD BUILT
TO REMEMBER.

Echoes of the Kingdom is a long-form fantasy universe shaped by story, memory, faith, power, and the search for what lies beyond the visible world.

WHY THIS WORLD EXISTS

When kingdoms fall,
memory remains.

Echoes of the Kingdom is not simply a fantasy novel. It is a world built to explore what happens when people inherit broken histories, divided kingdoms, and truths they can no longer fully remember.

Its stories move through war, power, responsibility, friendship, faith, and the choices that shape both individuals and civilizations.

Beneath all of them is one deeper question: what remains when the visible world is no longer enough?

AT THE HEART OF ECHOES

FOUR QUESTIONS.
ONE WORLD.

Beneath the kingdoms, wars, journeys, and histories, Echoes returns again and again to the same deeper themes.

FAITH

What do we follow when certainty disappears? What remains when sight is no longer enough?

MEMORY

What happens when a people forget where they came from— and begin building their future on fragments?

KINGDOM

Who should rule, why should others follow, and what kind of power can endure without destroying what it seeks to protect?

THE ECHO

What if the deepest truth has never disappeared— only our ability to hear it?

THE CREATOR

ONE STORY.

Echoes of the Kingdom began with a single story and a long-term vision: to build a living fantasy world where history, faith, power, memory, and choice are allowed to unfold slowly over time.

The work is written and developed by Michael He, with Echoes Studio serving as the home for everything that grows around the story.
