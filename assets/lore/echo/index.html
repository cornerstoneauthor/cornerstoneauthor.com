I

LISTEN

Every kingdom has a voice.

Every nation tells its own story.

Every heart follows something.

But beneath every voice...

There has always been another.

Be still.

II

SEE

Close your eyes.

Not to escape the world.

But to see beyond it.

The eyes reveal what is passing.

The heart begins to see what endures.

Look beyond what can be seen.

III

FOLLOW

Every journey begins with a single response.

Not because the Voice has only just begun.

But because,

at last,

someone listened.

Take the next step.
