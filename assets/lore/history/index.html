THE AGES OF ELARION

FROM THE BEGINNING TO THE RETURN

History is not the story of humanity rising toward glory, but of the world drifting from what it once knew—and of the Creator calling it home.

I

THE FIRST CHRONICLE

THE BEGINNING

“When the world still walked in harmony with the Light.”

Before kingdoms, division, and fear, the peoples of Elarion lived in the presence of the Creator. Every life answered its source, and the Echo was known by all.

II

THE SECOND CHRONICLE

THE SEPARATION

“The first silence began when the world turned away.”

The bond was broken. Distance entered the world, and what had once lived in unity began to divide, withdraw, and forget.

III

THE THIRD CHRONICLE

THE SILENCE

“The Echo never ceased. The world forgot how to listen.”

Generations passed beneath an apparent silence. Memory faded, truth fractured, and the peoples of Elarion preserved only fragments of what had once been whole.

IV

THE FOURTH CHRONICLE

THE RISE OF MAN

“Crowns were forged. Kingdoms were built. Wars began.”

Human ambition reshaped the Middle Lands. Cities, alliances, kingdoms, and empires rose beneath banners of order and glory, while division deepened beneath them.

This is the age in which the story begins.

V

THE FIFTH CHRONICLE

THE RETURN

“Every journey home begins with remembering.”

The Return is not the restoration of human glory. It is the calling of creation back toward the relationship, harmony, and truth that were present in the beginning.

The journey has only begun.
