THE LORE OF ELARION

KINGDOMS

There is a Kingdom beyond what the eye can see.

TWO WAYS OF SEEING

To the eye, kingdoms are made of walls, borders, armies, laws, crowns, and the people who live beneath them. They rise. They expand. They fracture. They disappear.

Elarion has known many such kingdoms, and it will know many more.

But the word kingdom carries another meaning in this world—one that cannot be measured on a map.

THE KINGDOM UNSEEN

Before crowns were forged and borders drawn, creation already belonged to its Creator.

That Kingdom was not founded by conquest, and it does not endure because an army protects it. It is older than every throne and larger than every civilization.

The peoples of Elarion may forget it. They may cease to see it. They may build other kingdoms and call them permanent.

Yet what is unseen is not therefore gone.

KINGDOMS OF THE EARTH

Human kingdoms and the realms of other peoples belong to history. Their rulers make choices. Their people remember victories and wounds. Their borders move with war, trade, marriage, fear, and ambition.

Some seek order. Some seek glory. Some seek survival. None remain unchanged forever.

THE KINGDOM THAT REMAINS

The deeper story of Elarion is not only about which earthly kingdom will rise next.

It is also about whether those who live within the visible world can still recognize the Kingdom that was never destroyed.

Kingdoms rise. Kingdoms fall. The Kingdom of the Creator remains.
