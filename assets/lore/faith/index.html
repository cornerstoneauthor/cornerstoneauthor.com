THE LORE OF ELARION

FAITH

Faith begins where belief becomes a road you are willing to enter.

MORE THAN BELIEF

Faith is not simply the question of whether someone believes the Creator exists.

Many in Elarion believe. Civilizations have built traditions, explanations, rituals, and entire ways of life around that belief.

But belief can remain at a distance. Faith cannot.

TO ENTER WHAT YOU BELIEVE

Faith begins when what is believed becomes something a person is willing to enter—when belief changes the next step, the next choice, the road one is willing to take.

The whole road is rarely visible beforehand.

Sometimes the next step becomes visible only after the last one has been taken.

A DIFFERENT KINGDOM

To those who stand outside it, the visible world may appear to be all that there is: kingdoms, dangers, ambitions, losses, and the struggle to endure.

But faith changes what can be seen—not because the visible world disappears, but because it is no longer the whole world.

To enter what one believes is to begin seeing another Kingdom within and beyond the one before the eyes.

THE ROAD

Faith does not remove uncertainty. It does not promise that every question will be answered before a choice must be made.

It asks something more difficult: whether a person will walk when enough has been shown for the next step.
