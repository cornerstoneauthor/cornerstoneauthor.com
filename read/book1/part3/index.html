PART III

WHERE ROADS MEET.

Two roads end at Riverport.

Aether, a hunter's son from Ashvale, followed a road of unreadable stones through the falling ash — past Whitestone, to a city he has never seen. Seren, a girl from a tidal village, followed a call no one else could hear, step by step across half the world, to the same gates.

They have never met. Neither knows the other exists.

But Riverport is not at peace. In the north, the power of Aster is on the move — and Aster's prize is Riverport itself. Bound by an old alliance, Whitestone stands with the city, and Riverport's lord has chosen the ground where the invasion must be met: a bridge at Riverstone, the strongest place to make a stand. A war of tens of thousands is coming — the first this forgotten age has seen.

And behind the war, the older question is spreading. In the northern woods the beasts are sick, the laws of the wild are failing, and the sickness is moving south along the roads — faster than the armies.

Two strangers walk into Riverport carrying pieces of the same map. No one has ever put the pieces together.
