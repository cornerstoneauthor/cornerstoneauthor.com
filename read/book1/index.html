ABOUT THIS BOOK

BEFORE THERE WAS AN EMPIRE.

In a fractured age, a child begins to see what others have accepted: divided lands, broken loyalties, and a world slowly forgetting what once held it together.

Book One follows the first steps of that journey—from a small valley and ordinary lives toward the beginnings of something far greater.
